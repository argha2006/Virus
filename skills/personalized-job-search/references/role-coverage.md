# Job types, designations and responsibility coverage

Use these generalized role families as an independent discovery input alongside the resume. They capture job types, designations, roles and responsibilities from user feedback without retaining source postings or facts. Search them even when the resume lacks the exact designation, platform, industry or responsibility. A title is a discovery signal; substantive responsibility overlap determines relevance. Search title variants and responsibility terms separately, then assess candidate fit without silently dropping relevant roles for missing resume evidence.

| Job type | Designation families | Responsibilities that establish target-role relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SAP planning architecture and delivery | Solution Architect, Functional Architect, Lead Consultant, Principal Consultant, Planning Solution Lead | Demand and supply planning, response planning, integrated business planning, solution design, configuration, implementation, ERP integration and planning-system roadmaps. Search relevant SAP IBP and APO modules independently. |
| Production planning and availability | PP/DS Architect or Consultant, Production Planning Lead, aATP/GATP Solution Lead | Detailed scheduling, capacity constraints, MRP, availability checks, allocation, backorder processing and integration between planning and execution. These roles need not also mention IBP. |
| Enterprise process design | Business Process Architect, Process Design Lead, Principal Planning Process Designer, Planning COE Lead | End-to-end planning design, process standards, capability roadmaps, cross-functional harmonization and translation of business needs into systems. |
| Process ownership and governance | Global Process Owner, Business Process Owner, IBP Process Lead, Planning Product Owner | Process governance, planning cadence, adoption, performance measures, deployment, optimization and coordination across functions and regions. Distinguish the IBP business process from the software product. |
| In-house planning applications | IT Application Expert, Application Specialist, Business Systems Analyst, Functional Analyst, Solution Owner | Business partnering, requirements, configuration, integrations, testing, migration, support, enhancements and application lifecycle ownership. Evaluate seniority from scope, not the absence of architect in the title. |
| Planning operations leadership | S&OP/SIOP Manager, Supply Planning Manager, Demand Planning Lead, Capacity or Inventory Planning Lead | Consensus planning, supply reviews, master planning, capacity and inventory decisions, operating performance and team leadership. Identify where direct operations ownership is mandatory. |
| Supply-chain transformation and advisory | Supply Chain Consultant, Senior Associate, Operations Consultant, Transformation Manager, Digital Supply Chain Lead | Planning operating models, manufacturing and supply-chain improvement, transformation roadmaps, stakeholder workshops and business change. Include advisory roles through these responsibilities even without matching consulting tenure in the resume. Exclude unrelated strategy, procurement-only or logistics-only work without substantive overlap with the target role families or resume. |
| Planning software product delivery | Solution Architect, Planning Consultant, Delivery Architect, Product Solution Lead | Planning models, enterprise implementation, demand forecasting, consensus planning, solution design and customer delivery. For adjacent platforms such as o9, Kinaxis and OMP, assess accepted alternatives and mandatory platform depth. |
| Service and spares planning | Service Supply Chain Architect, Spares Planning Lead, Aftermarket Planning Process Lead | Service-parts demand, installed-base or maintenance-driven forecasting, inventory, availability and service planning process design. Include through the role family and disclose domain gaps separately. |

For every in-scope family, vary business and technology vocabulary so that platform-neutral roles remain discoverable. Search demand, supply, production, capacity, inventory, S&OP/SIOP, governance and transformation responsibilities independently of resume keyword overlap. Relevant analytics, automation or AI requirements do not exclude a planning role when absent from the resume; mark the gap and claim candidate expertise only when demonstrated. Do not expand into unrelated software or AI jobs.

Do not save employers, requisition IDs, URLs, dates, historical availability, application statuses or candidate facts in this reference. Future feedback may refine these job types, designations and responsibility criteria without retaining the source examples.
