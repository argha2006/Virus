# Personalized Job Search

## Inputs and scope

Use two independent discovery inputs: the current user-designated resume and the generalized job types, designations, roles and responsibilities in [references/role-coverage.md](references/role-coverage.md). The coverage reference carries reusable patterns derived from user feedback without retaining the source examples. Read [references/resume-matching.md](references/resume-matching.md) to assess candidate fit after discovery, not to make the resume an exclusive search filter.

A role can enter the shortlist through either input; it need not match both. Include roles with substantive responsibility overlap with a target role family even when the exact title, platform, industry or leadership scope is absent from the resume. Explain the reason for inclusion and the candidate evidence or gaps separately. Explicit user exclusions and search constraints still apply; title similarity alone is insufficient.

Keep this skill reusable: do not store conversation-specific employers, job links or IDs, vacancy or application statuses, dates of observed postings, resume facts, personal identifiers or worked examples in the skill or its supporting files. Derive reusable role families and matching criteria from user feedback. Read candidate evidence and job facts afresh during each search; do not create a persistent candidate snapshot in the skill.

Default geography is India and outside India, reported separately. Prioritize senior roles with explicit requirements of at least 10 years, while retaining strong functional matches with lower or unstated requirements. Explicit current user preferences override these defaults.

## Discovery

Run a resume-led search and a separate role-family-led search, then merge and deduplicate the results. The second search must use the coverage reference's job types, designation variants and responsibilities without requiring resume keywords or demonstrated experience. Search all in-scope families, including titles that omit SAP or architect; do not stop after finding plentiful direct resume matches. Avoid a fixed employer list or exact-title whitelist.

Use live web search, job boards and employer career portals independently. Include regional sources for the locations being searched and observed applicant-tracking-system pages. Search broad functional terms before adding title, platform and location variants. Do not restrict discovery to recent postings; older requisitions may remain open.

For worldwide requests cover North America, UK/Ireland, continental Europe, Middle East and Asia-Pacific alongside India and India-remote. Keep a compact ledger of covered regions, sources, role families and access gaps. Complete planned coverage and a complementary expansion pass, verify the strongest leads, and deliver. Label incomplete coverage partial rather than continually expanding the search. Never claim an exhaustive list.

## Availability and supplied leads

Normalize job URLs and deduplicate by employer and requisition ID. For LinkedIn, resolve both job-view links and search links containing a selected job ID to the actual posting; search keywords are not job titles.

Read the actual description and capture title, employer, requisition, work location, arrangement, experience requirements, mandatory and preferred skills, responsibilities, posting date when shown, and application route. Search snippets are discovery evidence only.

If fetching fails, try the rendered browser page, then an exact requisition or title search and an observed employer link. A fetch error, blank dynamic page or blocked request does not establish closure. Do not guess URL variants. Keep unresolved availability separate.

An accessible description with a visible application route supports an apparently open classification; disclose if the destination was not checked. Fresh explicit closure overrides older indexed evidence. Do not submit an application to test availability. Do not invent posting dates.

For each user-supplied distinct lead, report relevant, stretch, closed, irrelevant or unable to verify, with a reason. Report application status only when observed during the current task. Separate already-applied roles from new opportunities and their counts; application activity alone does not prove the vacancy remains open.

## Ranking and classification

Keep availability, target-role relevance and candidate fit separate. Use the resume to rank and explain fit, not to automatically exclude target-role matches. Rank direct candidate matches by supported responsibility and required-skill overlap, then seniority, industry transferability and freshness. Also surface relevant role-family opportunities with limited or missing resume evidence in their own group. An experience threshold alone cannot make a role a strong candidate match.

Distinguish strong matches, relevant opportunities with experience caveats, and substantial qualification stretches. Preserve the exact total, domain and module-specific experience requirements and their AND/OR alternatives. A minimum is not an upper limit; a module minimum alone does not establish overqualification. Flag a bounded range below the candidate's demonstrated overall tenure without assuming the employer's flexibility.

Classify employers by the role's actual context:

- **GCC or Product/Manufacturing:** distinguish captive centers from direct product employers; a multinational office alone does not establish GCC status. Planning-software vendors remain product employers when delivering implementations for clients.
- **IT consulting:** external-client technology implementation or managed services. Default platform-led scope is SAP planning from either discovery input, including mixed-platform roles with gaps disclosed.
- **Management/Strategy consulting:** business, operations and supply-chain transformation advisory with meaningful planning or manufacturing overlap.
- **Other or Unclear:** recruiters, undisclosed clients or insufficient evidence. Do not assign the recruiter's client an assumed employer type.

Include adjacent planning-platform roles at product/manufacturing employers, GCCs and platform vendors when responsibilities match the target role families, even without demonstrated platform experience in the resume. Mark mandatory platform gaps explicitly. For IT consulting, the default platform-led scope remains SAP planning rather than non-SAP-only implementation roles; this is an employer/platform scope boundary, not a resume-evidence gate. Preserve any alternatives or equivalent-platform experience explicitly accepted by the description.

Use work location rather than headquarters. Preserve country restrictions on remote work and travel requirements. Do not infer work authorization, relocation willingness or sponsorship; relocation assistance alone does not establish sponsorship. List a multi-location requisition once.

## Output

State the check date, resume used, both discovery inputs, criteria and material access limitations. Lead with the strongest supported matches. Within India and outside India, group by employer category and distinguish direct resume matches, additional role-family matches and qualification stretches, with experience caveats where applicable. Give additional role-family matches clear visibility even when resume evidence is limited. Keep existing applications and unverified leads separate, with separate counts.

Use compact tables containing the role and direct link, employer/category, location/arrangement, stated experience, relevant responsibilities, inclusion basis (resume, role family or both), candidate fit and gaps, availability evidence and check date. Include posting dates when known and dispositions for supplied leads. Summarize both discovery tracks and region, employer-category and role-family coverage; an empty group means no verified match found in this search.

This skill searches and reports. Applications, resume uploads, recruiter contact, account creation, alert changes and recurring searches require a separate user request.
